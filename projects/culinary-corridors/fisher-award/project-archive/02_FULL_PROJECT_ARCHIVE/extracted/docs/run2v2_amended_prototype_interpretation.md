# Run 2 v2 Amended Prototype Interpretation

Created: 2026-04-29

## Executive interpretation

Run 2 v2 improves the project by narrowing inference and strengthening the geospatial argument. The global model remains valuable, but it should be presented as a discovery screen. The final Fisher claim should rest on focused spatial cases and geospatial residual analysis.

## Strong conclusions

1. **The project is viable as a geospatial analysis.** The amended prototype includes a filtered cuisine-similarity model, residual corridor maps, focused case maps, residual bridge scores, and boundary/connectivity proxy outputs.
2. **Cuisine similarity is spatially structured but not reducible to distance.** The filtered distance-only model has a negative log-distance coefficient (-0.1158) and R² of 0.3553, which means distance explains some but not all similarity.
3. **Scope discipline is necessary.** Generic-ingredient filtering changes residual rankings, so the global model should discover candidate corridors rather than prove mechanisms.
4. **East/Southeast Asia is the strongest inference case.** It contains six target cuisines and several positive residual links after filtering.
5. **Residual bridge scores provide the strongest geospatial-only addition.** They convert pairwise food-similarity residuals into mapped place-level roles using distance and coordinates.

## Iberian/Atlantic-Pacific findings

The secondary case remains visually and interpretively useful, but it should be framed as a corridor hypothesis. Its long-distance links are more vulnerable to platform bias and coarse geography. Use it as a contrast or secondary panel, not as the central proof.

## Cautious hypotheses

- Some long-distance residuals are consistent with maritime, migration, colonial, or trade corridor hypotheses.
- Some cuisines act as spatial bridges in the residual network.
- The boundary/permeability proxy suggests that residual strength differs by spatial grouping.

## Forbidden claims

- Do not claim that the model proves migration, trade, empire, or colonial causation.
- Do not treat cuisine labels as exact nation-state units.
- Do not claim the recipe corpus is representative of global cooking.
- Do not centralize fermentation or flavor chemistry without new validation.

## Fisher relevance

The amended prototype is Fisher-competitive because the map is not decoration. The spatial model produces the insight: distance baselines, residual corridors, focused regional maps, bridge scores, and boundary/permeability diagnostics reveal spatial structure that ingredient clustering alone cannot show.
