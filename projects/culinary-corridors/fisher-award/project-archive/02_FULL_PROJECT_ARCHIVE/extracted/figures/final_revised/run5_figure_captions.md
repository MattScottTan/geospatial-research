## Figure: `figures/final_revised/run5_east_se_asia_topographic_corridor_map.png`

**Title:** East/Southeast Asia Culinary Corridors on Relief Context

**Data and method:** The figure uses the Run 4 v2 East/Southeast Asia residual/accessibility metrics from `data/processed/run4v2_east_se_asia_accessibility_metrics.csv`, cuisine coordinates from `data/crosswalks/cuisine_geo_crosswalk.csv`, and a local Basemap ETOPO relief image (`etopo1.jpg`) as cartographic relief context. Corridor line widths are scaled by positive residual cuisine similarity after a distance baseline. Orange lines mark same-subregion links, teal lines mark island/maritime-context links, and thin lines are contextual lower-residual links included for spatial reading.

**What it shows:** The strongest focused-case links — especially Thai–Vietnamese and Chinese–Korean, with Filipino links as maritime/island context — become easier to read when placed against coastlines, islands, peninsulas, mountain/plateau context, and the South China Sea.

**What it does not prove:** The map does not prove that topography, maritime movement, migration, trade, or historical exchange caused the observed residuals. It is not a measured least-cost path model or terrain-friction model. It is a relief-context map for interpreting residual culinary geography.

**Recommended placement:** Main supporting figure directly after the East/Southeast Asia focused-case figure in the StoryMap/report. It should supplement, not replace, the residual bridge-index figure.

## Figure: `figures/final_revised/run5_corridor_callout_or_inset.png`

**Title:** Corridor callouts: where focused residuals become spatially legible

**Data and method:** Uses the same residual/accessibility metrics, cuisine coordinates, and relief background as the primary Run 5 map. The left panel highlights the Thai–Vietnamese mainland adjacency context. The right panel highlights Filipino links across a maritime/island setting in Southeast Asia.

**What it shows:** The callout figure provides a zoomed reading of two spatial situations that are harder to see in the full regional map: a compact mainland residual link and a maritime/island residual cluster.

**What it does not prove:** The callouts remain contextual. They do not establish a causal mechanism or measured route.

**Recommended placement:** Optional appendix/supporting figure, or a StoryMap sidecar/callout if the layout allows a visual zoom after the main Run 5 relief map.
