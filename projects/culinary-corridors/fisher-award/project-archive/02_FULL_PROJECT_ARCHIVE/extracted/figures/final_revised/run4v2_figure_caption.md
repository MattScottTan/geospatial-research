# Run 4 v2 Figure Caption

**Figure:** `figures/final_revised/run4v2_topographic_corridor_map.png`

East/Southeast Asia residual culinary links with a corridor-accessibility proxy. The left panel maps cuisine anchors and residual links; line width represents positive residual strength after the distance baseline, and solid lines indicate higher corridor-accessibility scores. Squares indicate island or archipelago cuisines. The right panel compares each pair's residual cuisine similarity with the corridor-accessibility proxy. The strongest residual links, including Thai-Vietnamese and Chinese-Korean, also sit in high-accessibility same-subregion contexts, while Filipino links highlight the importance of maritime/coastal positioning in the focused case. The proxy combines same-subregion status, pairwise distance, coastal/island context, maritime context, and transparent barrier flags. It is not a measured terrain-cost, migration, trade, or historical route model.
