## Past-winner patterns to emulate
The playbook and feature matrix indicate that strong Fisher projects repeatedly do the following:

1. Reveal hidden geography: destruction, exposure, access, fragmentation, change, or under-seen spatial structure.
2. Use GIS operations as the engine of the result, not as decoration.
3. Document a credible data stack with source, scale, preprocessing, and uncertainty.
4. Pair maps with quantitative metrics: rankings, residuals, validation, sensitivity checks, or model output.
5. Lead with a hero map and a guided visual argument.
6. Preserve claim discipline: strong maps, modest causal language.
