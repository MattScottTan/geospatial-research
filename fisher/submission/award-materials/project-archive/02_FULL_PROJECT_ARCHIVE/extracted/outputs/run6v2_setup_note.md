# Run 6 v2 Setup Note

Created: 2026-05-01

## Purpose

Run 6 v2 expands the existing concise ArcGIS StoryMap conversion into an atlas-style StoryMap script. The goal is not to redo the Culinary Corridors analysis; it is to make the submission self-contained, explanatory, and easier for a Fisher Award reviewer to follow without needing to open the technical PDF.

## How this differs from Run 6

Run 6 produced a concise copy-paste package with headings, figure placements, captions, callouts, source notes, and QA instructions. Run 6 v2 keeps the same approved figures and claim hierarchy, but expands the prose to match the explanatory depth of the provided StoryMap exemplar, *Cloudy with a Chance of Compute*. The new version adds a longer introduction, a staged atlas-method section, numbered findings, deeper figure explanations, cuisine-pair vignettes, a fuller limitations section, and an expanded source/method note.

## What the exemplar teaches

The exemplar opens with a concrete spatial contrast, builds a clear atlas workflow, structures results as numbered findings, explains methods in accessible language, uses case studies to make statistical patterns tangible, and ends with sources and limitations. Run 6 v2 translates those structural lessons to Culinary Corridors without copying its wording, subject matter, statistics, or citations.

## Inherited scope

The final scope remains unchanged: global discovery screen, East/Southeast Asia as the primary focused case, Iberian/Atlantic-Pacific as a secondary or diagnostic case, topography/relief as spatial context, and residual bridge scores as the strongest geospatial-only insight.

## Non-goals

No new data, figures, models, or statistical results are created. No hosted StoryMap is produced. The expanded text must not claim global representativeness, cuisine-to-nation exactness, or causality from migration, trade, terrain, maritime routes, or colonial history.

## Immediate risks

The main risks are over-writing the StoryMap into a dense paper, inventing unsupported historical mechanisms, or making the relief map sound causal. This run mitigates those risks through modular paste blocks, claim-safety language, and an explicit readability audit.
