# StoryMap Callout Boxes

Use these as emphasized quote, side note, or callout blocks in ArcGIS StoryMaps.

## Research question
**When does cuisine similarity follow geographic distance, and when does it follow corridors, bridges, regions, or boundaries instead?**

## Method
**Residual = observed cuisine similarity − distance-predicted cuisine similarity.** Positive residuals become candidate culinary corridors.

## Key finding
Cuisine similarity is spatially structured, but not reducible to distance.

## Global caution
Global = discovery, not proof.

## Focused case
East/Southeast Asia is the strongest focused inference case because it is spatially coherent and supported by residual links after filtering.

## Topographic caution
The relief map provides spatial context; it does not prove that terrain, coastlines, or maritime routes caused the cuisine similarities.

## Geospatial contribution
The residual bridge index turns pairwise food similarities into mapped place-level roles. This insight requires geography.

## Final contribution
GIS transforms cuisine similarity from a list of shared ingredients into a map of residual corridors, bridges, and spatial interpretation.
