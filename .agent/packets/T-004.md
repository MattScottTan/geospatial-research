# Relevant Decisions

- **D-003** The audience is a numerate reader — algebra and reading a graph may be assumed,
  **nothing statistical**. Any impulse to write "recall that" or "as is well known" about a
  statistical concept must instead become a definition. Length is expected and acceptable.
- **D-005** `.tex` authored with Write/Edit only. Bash heredocs corrupt backslashes here.
- **D-006** The primer is `\input` into both papers and must not assume either paper's
  specific context. Paper-specific background belongs in the paper.

# Relevant Memory

- Verified LaTeX packages: `amsmath, amssymb, booktabs, graphicx, hyperref, natbib,
  geometry`. Stick to these.
- Definitions the papers depend on being *exactly* right:
  - Moran's I = `(n/S0) * (z'Wz)/(z'z)` where `z = y - mean(y)` and `S0 = sum of all
    entries of W`. It measures whether neighbouring values **co-vary**.
  - Getis-Ord Gi\* compares a neighbourhood's **sum** against what it would be if the
    neighbourhood held the **global mean**, standardised by the **global** standard
    deviation. It measures **level**, not co-variation. Gi\* includes the focal unit;
    plain Gi does not.
  - **This level-versus-covariation distinction is the theoretical core of Paper 1.** It
    must be developed carefully enough that a reader can predict, before being told, which
    statistic a shift in one region's mean will disturb.
  - A permutation test builds its null by reshuffling observed values across locations, so
    it assumes **exchangeability**. Paper 2 turns on the fact that this assumption also
    quietly requires the weights matrix to have been fixed in advance.

# Constraints

- Build every concept from an intuition **before** any formula. Formula second, always.
- Use a running concrete example rather than abstract notation where possible.
- Explain *why* each object is defined the way it is, not only what it is — a reader should
  finish understanding why Moran's I has that particular ratio form.
- Do not assume prior exposure to matrices. Introduce a matrix as a table of numbers with
  rules, and an eigenvector as a direction the matrix only stretches.
- Do not smuggle in either paper's finding. The primer sets up the machinery; the papers
  deliver the results.
- Every term listed in AC-003 must be defined and must appear in the glossary with its
  defining section.
