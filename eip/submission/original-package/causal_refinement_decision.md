# Causal Refinement Decision

Date: 2026-03-14 12:01 PM America/New_York

## Decision

**NO-GO** on further causal escalation in the main submission workflow.

## Why

1. Stage 4 already shows that the simple cross-sectional sign weakens or flips under stricter within-place comparisons.
2. Stage 5 delivers a useful pilot panel, but not a persuasive treatment effect.
3. Stage 6 broadens the panel and still fails to produce a stable, submission-grade causal estimate.
4. Additional escalation is unlikely to improve the EIP submission more than originality, case-study explanation, and StoryMap polish will.

## What to do instead

- Freeze the causal boundary clearly.
- Convert the mixed causal results into a strength: the project is careful enough **not** to overclaim.
- Use the four case studies and the originality layer to explain why compute matters as part of an infrastructure bundle rather than as a universal deterministic driver.

## Operational consequence

The conditional task to create one more causal refinement output package remains intentionally unexecuted and should be treated as superseded by this decision unless new evidence radically changes the landscape.
