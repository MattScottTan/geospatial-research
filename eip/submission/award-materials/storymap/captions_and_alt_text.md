# Captions and Alt Text

## Global atlas media

### fig_access_map
- **Caption:** Distance from each city in the 8,000-city frame to the nearest AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud region.
- **Alt text:** Global point map showing cities colored by distance to the nearest major cloud region; shorter distances cluster in North America, Europe, and East Asia, while many cities in Africa and parts of Latin America are much farther away.

### fig_ai_map
- **Caption:** Observed AI-related research activity in the delivered OpenAlex-derived city overlay.
- **Alt text:** Global point map of cities with observed AI-related research activity, with dense clusters in East Asia, Europe, and North America.

### fig_hist
- **Caption:** AI-linked cities sit much closer to major cloud regions than the broader city system.
- **Alt text:** Histogram comparing distance-to-cloud distributions for all 8,000 cities and for AI-linked cities; the AI-linked distribution is shifted toward shorter distances.

### fig_weighted
- **Caption:** When the overlay is weighted by AI works, the center of gravity moves even closer to cloud infrastructure.
- **Alt text:** Chart showing that the weighted distribution of AI activity is concentrated at shorter distances from cloud regions than the unweighted distribution.

### fig_hotspots
- **Caption:** Hot spots and cold spots show that the relationship is spatially structured, not just anecdotal.
- **Alt text:** World map with highlighted hot spots and cold spots of AI-related activity; only a limited number of statistically strong clusters appear.

### fig_priority
- **Caption:** The priority-city screen highlights large cities that combine weak compute proximity with no observed AI works in the delivered overlay.
- **Alt text:** World map of the top-priority cities, with many concentrated in Africa, Latin America, and parts of Southeast Asia.

### fig_coef_compare
- **Caption:** The distance coefficient remains negative in both spatial models, although it shrinks once geography is modeled more explicitly.
- **Alt text:** Simple coefficient comparison chart with two negative distance estimates and two positive population estimates.

### fig_quadrants
- **Caption:** The quadrant view separates alignment cases, exception cases, and priority-city cases.
- **Alt text:** Scatter or quadrant plot dividing cities by compute access and AI activity into four analytic groups.

## Originality media

### fig_bundle_map
- **Caption:** The Compute Opportunity Bundle Index extends the atlas beyond raw distance by combining proximity, provider diversity, redundancy, institutional anchors, and city scale.
- **Alt text:** Global point map of the top 1,000 cities by population colored by bundle score, with strongest scores concentrated in major metropolitan corridors across Europe, East Asia, and North America.

### fig_bundle_vs_distance
- **Caption:** Distance-only access and bundle opportunity are related but not identical.
- **Alt text:** Scatterplot comparing bundle score against a distance-only access score, with annotated cities showing cities that rise or fall under the richer bundle measure.

### fig_bundle_top_cities
- **Caption:** The top bundle cities are places where multiple opportunity layers reinforce one another.
- **Alt text:** Horizontal bar chart ranking the top cities by compute opportunity bundle score.
